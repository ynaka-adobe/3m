Clean the substrate

Remove oils and release agents with isopropyl alcohol. Allow to flash off fully.

Apply at temperature

Bond above 21 °C where possible. Below 10 °C, use a primer.

Apply firm pressure

Pressure, not time, drives initial wet-out. Roll the full length of the joint.

Allow to build

Frequently asked questions

Built on native <details> — keyboard operable and findable by browser search with no JS.

How long before a bonded joint reaches full strength?

Handling strength is typically reached within 20 minutes. Around 90% of ultimate strength develops over 72 hours at ambient temperature, and full cure at seven days.

Heat accelerates this — one hour at 65 °C approximates three days at ambient.

Can tapes be applied below freezing?

Not directly. Below 10 °C the adhesive is too firm to wet out the substrate. Use a primer rated for low temperature application, or warm the substrate locally.

Which surfaces need a primer?

Low surface energy plastics almost always do:

  • Polypropylene and polyethylene
  • PTFE and other fluoropolymers
  • Powder-coated surfaces with a silicone-containing finish
Is the bond reversible?

Structural acrylic bonds are designed to be permanent. Removal normally requires cutting the bond line with a serrated tool, followed by solvent cleanup of residue.
